Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location (city/sub-city)
  • User type (Donor, Recipient, NGO)

Donation/Request Information

  • Item descriptions and photos
  • Location information
  • Contact preferences

Usage Data

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited
  • Time and date of visits

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Match donors with recipients
  • Verify NGO credentials
  • Communicate about donations and requests
  • Improve our platform
  • Send updates and notifications (with your consent)
  • Ensure platform security

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only:

  • With matched donors/recipients (limited contact info)
  • With verified NGO partners (when relevant)
  • When required by law
  • With your explicit consent

We never sell your personal information.

4. Data Storage

Currently, HelpEthiopia stores data locally in your browser using localStorage. This means:

  • Your data stays on your device
  • We don't have access to your personal information
  • Clearing browser data will delete your information

Note: Future versions may include server-side storage with enhanced security.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Opt-out of communications
  • Export your data

6. Security

We implement security measures to protect your information, including:

  • User verification processes
  • Secure data transmission
  • Regular security audits
  • Limited data access

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes via email or platform notification.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or concerns, contact us at:
privacy@helpethiopia.org
