Simple Steps to Smart Giving

Inspired by Freecycle's concept, our process is streamlined for maximum impact and minimal fuss.

🎁

1. Post It

Donors quickly list their items (food, books, electronics) with photos and location details.

📍

2. Pin the Need

Recipients/NGOs post requests, marking their location and urgency on the Live Map.

🤝

3. Smart Match

The platform matches nearby donations to active requests based on need and location.

✅

4. Track & Complete

Users confirm and track the exchange until the item is safely delivered to the recipient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about HelpEthiopia

How does HelpEthiopia work?

HelpEthiopia connects donors with verified recipients and NGOs. Simply post what you want to donate or request what you need, and our platform matches you with nearby opportunities. Track everything from posting to delivery.

Is it free to use?

Yes! HelpEthiopia is completely free for both donors and recipients. Our mission is to make giving accessible to everyone in Ethiopia.

How do I verify my NGO?

NGOs can apply for verification by submitting official documentation through our signup process. Our team reviews each application to ensure legitimacy and transparency.

What items can I donate?

You can donate food, clothes, books, electronics, hygiene products, and more. We accept both new and gently used items that can help those in need.

How is my safety ensured?

All users go through verification, transactions are tracked, and we provide secure communication channels. Our support team is available 24/7 to address any concerns.
